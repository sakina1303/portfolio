About

Curiosity turned into craft.

I started by wanting to understand how products work, not just how to ship code. That curiosity became a habit of looking at problems from the user’s side first, then designing the simplest way to solve them.

I enjoy building with a product lens: what is actually useful, what reduces friction, what creates delight, and what can scale without losing clarity. That mindset shows up in the interfaces I design, the systems I build, and the teams I like working with.

I care about AI when it makes experiences more personal and more accessible, not just more impressive. I’m drawn to products where thoughtful interaction design and solid engineering create something that feels calm, useful, and memorable.
